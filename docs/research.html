Research

I study ice sheets dynamics, model parameter sensitivity and inference, and processes which impact sea level rise contributions. My work includes exploring assumptions of ice rheology, coupled ice–ocean processes controlling ice shelf channelization, and future projections of West Antarctic ice loss.
